# gre-words
Collection of GRE words with their meanings, examples and origin.

**Study here:** https://mkebiclioglu.github.io/gre-words/

A spaced-repetition flashcard app (like Anki) built on `GRE Vocab Prep.txt`:

- **Study**: rate each word Again / Hard / Good / Easy, and it comes back on an Anki-style schedule. 15 new words a day by default.
- **Words**: search all words and meanings, and see each word's status and next review.
- **Progress**: words mastered, streak, study-day calendar, upcoming reviews, recall rate, and your trickiest words with a drill mode.

Progress is saved on the device. On iPhone, add it to the Home Screen and save a backup now and then from the Progress tab.

New words appended to `GRE Vocab Prep.txt` (same format) show up in the app automatically.

You can still import `GRE Vocab Prep.txt` into Anki desktop if you prefer.
